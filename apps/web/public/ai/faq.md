# Nexyyra Events — FAQ

## Who is Nexyyra Events?
Nexyyra Events is the trade name of Nexyyra Events and Promotions Private Limited (CIN U70200ME2026PTC476014), incorporated in 2026, with its registered office in Telhara, Maharashtra and a Delivery & Coordination Office in Pune. It plans, designs and produces weddings, corporate events and celebrations across India.

## What wedding planning services does Nexyyra provide?
Full-service wedding planning: venue shortlisting, décor and floral design, vendor contracts, guest hospitality, sangeet and reception production, destination wedding logistics and on-the-day management.

## How much does wedding planning cost?
Collections start from ₹10 Lakhs (50–150 guests), ₹35 Lakhs (150–500 guests) and ₹1 Crore+ (500+ guests). Single services start from ₹2 Lakhs. Every proposal is itemised after a free consultation.

## What corporate event services are available?
Conferences, AGMs, product launches, award nights, dealer meets, brand activations, exhibitions and galas — with AV production, hybrid streaming, stage design, and a post-event debrief and media hand-over.

## What celebrity event services are offered?
Talent and artist booking coordination through the artist's management, red-carpet and media arrangements, security liaison and VIP hospitality.

## Can I book a single service, like décor or production only?
Yes. Any one service can be booked on its own; single services start from ₹2 Lakhs.
